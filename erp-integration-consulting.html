The Problem With Generic ERP

Most ERP implementations fail not because the software is wrong, but because it was configured to match the vendor's template rather than the company's actual operating model. The result is a system your finance team works around, not with — manual exports, reconciliation spreadsheets, and workarounds that accumulate year after year.

PMG Services approaches ERP integration differently. We start with how your business actually operates — your revenue model, cost structure, reporting requirements, and decision cycles — and build the integration layer to serve that reality precisely.

What We Build

  • ERP to BI integration pipelines (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite → Power BI)
  • Automated month-end and year-end close workflows
  • Invoice processing automation with OCR and validation rules
  • Custom financial reporting engines and dashboards
  • Intercompany reconciliation automation
  • Treasury and liquidity management tooling
  • Data warehouse architecture (Snowflake, Azure)
  • Machine learning models for forecast and anomaly detection

Our Integration Approach

Every engagement starts with a two-week discovery phase: we map your current data flows from source systems to reporting outputs, identify where manual steps introduce risk or delay, and document the business logic that needs to be preserved or improved.

From that map, we design an integration architecture that connects your ERP, accounting, and planning systems into a unified data layer — with automated pipelines replacing manual extraction, and governance rules ensuring data quality at every step.

We build with your team's capabilities in mind. Every system we deliver is documented, tested, and handed over with training so your team can own and extend it without ongoing consultant dependency.

Who This Is For

Our ERP integration and custom financial systems work is best suited for mid-market companies that are growing into complexity — businesses that have outgrown their current setup but are not yet large enough for a full SAP S/4HANA implementation with a Big Four systems integrator.

We also work with larger organizations that need a focused, fast-moving team to build a specific integration or automation layer alongside a broader ERP rollout — without the overhead and timeline of a full consulting house engagement.

Typical Engagement Results

  • Month-end close reduced from 3 weeks to under 1 week
  • Finance team time reclaimed from reporting to analysis
  • Single source of truth for financial data across systems
  • Real-time P&L and cash visibility for management
  • System documentation enabling internal ownership
